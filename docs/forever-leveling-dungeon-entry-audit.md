# Five-player dungeon entry and route order

## Current policy

The primary route is five Humans playing Warrior tank, Priest healer, Mage, Rogue and Warlock together. Wailing Caverns is required, including its three neutral quests, Naralex event and outside returns. A saved older opt-out cannot remove that visit. Other editions retain their existing behavior until separately reviewed.

Place dungeons when the party can clear their required encounters and collect the selected quests. Do not add slow outdoor work merely to reach an old recommended level. A genuine quest prerequisite, access requirement or unsafe encounter still blocks departure.

- Ordinary enemies and dungeon trash may be at most four levels above the lowest participating player before combat.
- A captured dungeon boss with a matching encounter identity may be at most five levels above the lowest participant. The forecast and native export enforce this before combat; an elite rank or a boss-like name is insufficient.
- Selected quest pickup minima and prerequisite chains still apply.
- XP from a later kill, dungeon or hand-in cannot fund an earlier departure.
- Count one-time quest rewards once. Keep the forty-slot quest journal limit.

## Evidence and limits

The entry audit uses the existing literal Questie Forever NPC export and captured build-70205 client maps, areas, encounters and boss identities. It parses data without executing provider Lua or calling the crawler. Client artifact and provider hashes are checked before joining records.

AreaTable records bind dungeon subareas to their instance map even where the map's own area field is zero. Provider `zoneID` and spawn-area bindings are both retained. Explicit Alliance-friendly NPCs are listed separately and excluded from combat floors; records without that flag remain conservative. Boss identities use a direct creature ID or a globally unambiguous exact name with the correct encounter context.
