# Source-backed five-player collection preparation

## Qualified starter selection and text-only reminders (local v84)

Personal starters use a one-player collection budget, rather than multiplying their quota by five. A starter-zone location alone does not justify an unsupported or over-budget farm. Nonessential descendant errands cannot force that farm back into the selected route; required class unlocks and dungeon prerequisites remain protected. Missing reference data preserves the prior personal flow rather than pretending that a collection is known to be cheap. The shared five-player budget keeps its existing meaning.

An immediately following `.disablecheckbox` marks its source quest phase as a text-only reminder. It cannot claim quest acceptance, objective completion, a hand-in or extra combat XP. The real later phase remains available. Native output retains both the reminder and its disabling control when displaying the source step. Current numerical scope and incomplete route funding are in the [audit](forever-leveling-no-grind-audit-2026-10-31.md#current-status).

## Source-directed material combat (local v79)

`compileCampaignMaterialWork` binds each collection to its exact archived source step, full `.collect` target, corrected quest quantity and explicitly named self-gather creature. Buying, use-item steps, multiple possible named sources, supplied items and unresolved methods do not earn assumed gathering XP. Matching adjacent blocking collection and objective programs share one action; different guards, owners, contexts and text-only counters stay separate. Existing bookmarks resolve to the retained action.

`forecastCampaignMaterialCombat` models a conditional fresh-character reference: level 1, empty XP bar, no recorded bags or quest history, no outside item transfers and no earlier fight against any possible source of those materials. A zero inventory lower bound on an existing character is **not** proof of an empty bag. Anchors, custom combat inputs, partial or unequal stock and unresolved earlier loot keep acquisition unmodeled. Already sufficient stock requires no second gathering workload. Foreign references, unknown ranks and unavailable rates keep their holds.

For one source creature, required quest kills and material completion use one stopping workload, `max(required kills, material completion)`. They are never added as independent fights. Two items require compatible joint-roll evidence. A future shared-kill counter may advance only if its remainder is guaranteed complete; random partial counters need a joint inventory model and remain held. Computation is atomic: an unresolved later source does not leak partial XP or counters from an earlier source.

These fights do not create a synthetic quest or accept a real quest early. Every player still has **40 slots**, completed-unhanded quests still occupy slots, and pending selected dungeon quests remain reserved. Collecting an ordinary item before pickup is allowed only with explicit non-quest drop context; quest-only items require their actual pickup.

The initial archived Human reference now counts eight Stonetusk Boars for four Chunks of Boar Meat per separate starter, or 38 pooled kills for the five-person shared starter. The conditional combat estimates are 560–592 XP per separate player and 532 XP per shared player. These are expected-workload references, not observed inventory, guaranteed completion times or current-server payouts. `item-acquisition-unqualified` remains visible; this feature does not qualify the complete route. Full-review results are recorded in the [current audit](forever-leveling-no-grind-audit-2026-10-31.md).

## Qualification

`campaign-funding-collections.ts` builds a temporary planning view from the matching source archive, quest catalog and combat references. It changes no captured or historical inputs. Matching build, provider revision, quest/NPC/item export hashes and archive identity are required.

A candidate needs:

- An unrestricted Alliance quest with a complete source pickup, objective and hand-in. Class restrictions, unresolved requirements, repeatability and unsupported interaction requirements stay held.
- Exactly one item objective, one explicit creature target, a known quantity and supported five-player sharing/drop/stack evidence. Provided tools, ground objects, creature-credit mixtures, bosses, rares and unknown enemy levels are excluded from this pass.
- A quest-only drop reference and an item identity used by only that quest in the modeled references. Ordinary items or items reused by another quest cannot establish fresh inventory from an unstarted quest state.
- Source coordinates in a recognized zone for every phase. World coordinates stay unchanged. NPC/item use, gossip, timers, unrelated quest-state guards and unsupported commands are held.

The candidate view is not the route. Selection must also satisfy the actual pickup levels, prerequisite closure, exclusivity, known reward facts, travel and reached party configurations. The [40-slot quest-log limit](forever-leveling-quest-log.md) applies to every participating character. Only full five-player work can use this pass; personal starter work is unchanged.

Source quest-state guards must be true before the proposed phase. A completed parent handed in at the same NPC no longer remains active when a split child pickup runs. When that source hand-in and prerequisite are explicit, the native compiler changes the child's copied `isQuestComplete` guard to `isQuestTurnedIn`; the original compound interaction retains its original guard. Unrelated or unsupported guards do not qualify a candidate. The inspected installed source is RXPGuides v4.11.29; the configured v4.11.27 export target still requires in-game validation.

`campaign-source-cleanup.ts` distinguishes a later optional cleanup from an instruction to abandon work before its hand-in. A collection can pass this distinction only when its complete selected lifecycle precedes every matching abandonment in the source chronology. Cleanup steps must explicitly be optional and contain only supported cleanup annotations. Early or mandatory abandonment, unknown conditions, runtime guards, mixed operations, later restarted work and mismatched source identities remain held. The broad source-library abandonment marker stays intact for other consumers. No abandonment is added to the selected lifecycle or native export.

For Love Eternal (963) meets that source distinction: the earlier Darkshore lifecycle is complete before the later optional cleanup. Its reference cost is one shared quest-only drop for all five players, not five pooled drops. Selection still requires the level-16 target to fit the reached party's enemy band. The earlier level-11 Ameth'Aran visit does not satisfy that condition. Patrol availability, turn-in roleplay and the placement of a later southern excursion require separate journey review; this qualification supplies no early XP or travel-time guarantee.

## Complete-chain cost and XP

The existing loot calculator supplies the reference expected and slow kill budgets for all five inventories. One pooled item per player at a guaranteed one-item drop needs five group kills; one guaranteed copied item needs one. Unknown sharing is not replaced with either assumption.

Every newly required collection in the prerequisite closure must qualify. Their combined budgets must stay within the existing **20 expected / 40 slow kills**. Two individually eligible 15-kill collections therefore fail as a 30-kill chain. A cheap final trophy cannot import an unresolved or expensive personal-drop parent. A prerequisite already rewarded by all participants is not paid or priced again.

The pass accepts fresh, unstarted collections only. Accepted, partially completed or mixed-completion inventories are held instead of being restarted at a full quota. Source prerequisites remain attached to the pickup and participate in closure planning.

Generated preparation retains the source instructions, phase identity and exact waypoints. It uses stable phase IDs and existing bookmark aliases. New work is optional once its own target is met; an accepted quest can still be finished. Existing required source work retains its required status.

Combat and rewards run through the existing continuous per-player XP ledger. The collection selector supplies no bonus XP, extra dungeon population or compensation reward. Reference loot budgets and their resulting XP are planning estimates, not guaranteed current-server payouts or a measured expectation over all possible loot histories.

## Travel and configuration checks

Source world coordinates remain available to the website and native addon. The compiler does not invent map percentages or world-map dimensions. A same-zone segment with incomparable coordinate systems receives a dimensionless penalty equal to the largest normalized map displacement. Identical world positions on the same floor have zero displacement; ordinary map-percentage scoring and inter-zone journeys retain their existing handling.

This penalty is a selection heuristic. It is not a travel-time estimate, an upper bound on path length, or proof of a safe route. Exact road/transport work and complete-journey timing still belong to the [five-player speed objective](forever-leveling-five-player-speed.md).

The new pass follows the previously selected allocation. Its proposal is checked across every supported configuration. Newly proposed combat must fit the lowest reached player within the existing three-level preparation band. Earlier unsupported tasks or larger deficits at the same task reject the proposal. Unknown work beyond an earlier blocker is not evidence of a reachable task.
