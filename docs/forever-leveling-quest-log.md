# Five-player quest-log capacity

WoW Forever permits at most **40 active quests per character**. The guide treats that owner-confirmed limit as a hard constraint. It applies separately to all five players, including personal starter and class work, dungeon preparation, optional quests and completed objectives awaiting hand-in.

The local v79 source-material review checks **2,640 default-character** and **160 mixed-character** complete-itinerary bounds, with peaks of **20/40** and **19/40**. The 528 default settings cover all 33 race/class editions; the 32 mixed settings cover four explicit parties. These are conservative itinerary bounds even when a forecast stops earlier at an XP gate. They do not imply the route is XP-funded or that an unavailable future hand-in has already happened.

Source-directed gathering does not create a synthetic quest or accept its delivery quest early. An ordinary collection before pickup earns only a conditional reference workload; its quest starts occupying a slot only at its real pickup. The new full-log regression holds 20 accepted plus 20 completed-unhanded quests: the next pickup is rejected, no reward is paid, and used/peak remain 40. Existing regressions retain selected unidentified dungeon reservations and reject a forty-first quest. Restoring quest 418's mandatory later hand-in frees its slot only at that planned hand-in, never at its earlier blocked executable frontier.

## Slot accounting

- `accepted` and `objectives-complete` each occupy one slot. Completing an objective does not free space.
- `rewarded` and `not-started` occupy no slots. The rewarded quest history is not limited to 40 records.
- Helping with another player’s shared kills does not accept that quest or occupy a slot; a character with 40 active quests can still receive supported kill XP.
- Hand in before accepting a follow-up when the log is full. Quest changes within one NPC interaction are processed in their original order.
- An already-held quest occupies one slot even if the guide mentions it again. A mapped guaranteed-dungeon quest shares that same slot.
- Each selected unidentified guaranteed-dungeon quest reserves one slot from its entry checkpoint through the modeled hand-in. Four selected slots require room for four quests. These reservations are planning entries, not invented quest IDs or observed acceptances.

The planner never automatically abandons a quest or deletes saved states. An inconsistent legacy report above 40 remains available for correction, but its forecast is held. Loading an actual-XP correction does not waive the quest-log limit. Starting a section after the dungeon entry check cannot bypass its capacity requirement.

## Interactive reporting and continuous forecasts

`quest-log.ts` owns the shared capacity rule and the local reservation ledger. Campaign pickup reporting and the detailed dungeon status editor reject a new active quest when the character has no free slot. The UI disables those new active statuses and explains that a hand-in frees space. Existing held quests can still be completed and handed in; saved reports can still be corrected.

`campaign-xp.ts` checks the same limit at each reached pickup and before reserving a guaranteed bundle. A capacity blocker supplies no XP and cannot be filled using later rewards. Per-member forecast output includes the current modeled occupancy and its cumulative peak so far. That known-prefix peak alone does not certify the remainder of a route after an unrelated XP blocker.

The application only knows recorded quest states. Quests accepted outside the guide must be recorded before their slots can be accounted for. No automatic in-game quest-log scan is claimed.

## Complete-itinerary audit

`auditCampaignQuestLog` checks the entire selected action order independently of XP reachability. It respects personal action ownership, party work, route selections and selected dungeon slots. It includes all potentially needed optional pickups, widens duplicate lifetimes from the earliest pickup through the latest hand-in, and retains unmatched pickups through the route end. A quest with a missing pickup is conservatively counted as carried from the beginning and reported under `inferredCarriedQuestIds`.

This produces a conservative upper bound for each player's recorded quest log and the recognized conditional source branches. A known skipped or uncertain hand-in cannot free a slot. A potentially reachable conditional pickup is included, even when another branch skips it. Quest history and partial objective progress are widened when unrecorded, rather than inventing certain completion or reward history. Correlated branches can therefore yield a bound larger than any single execution. A bound above 40 is a review failure; it does not assert that every optional execution overflows. The audit never treats abandoned quests as a way to pass.

`campaign-source-conditions.ts` selects the same normalized controls as native lowering. `campaign-quest-log-conditions.ts` tracks possible quest states for six quest-state predicates, `.itemcount` and `.money`. Unknown item counts and currency stay unknown. Explicit provider objective indices prevent one completed sub-objective from proving the entire quest ready. This scope excludes arbitrary unrecorded outside quests, other addon controls and game execution.

`scripts/review-leveling-xp-funding.mjs` includes this audit for each configuration. Its summary exposes `questLogCapacityPassed` and `maximumQuestLogUpperBound`, with individual peaks and the first potential overflow action in each result. A capacity failure exits 2 even without `--require-funded`. XP funding and the arithmetic witness remain separate results.

The [current no-grinding audit](forever-leveling-no-grind-audit-2026-10-31.md) records the accepted matrix and remaining funding gaps. Passing quest-log capacity does not establish a funded 1–60 route, accurate live payouts, connected travel or the under-48-hour goal.
