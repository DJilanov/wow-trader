The baseline can explicitly opt in to the owner's **24–30 XP per player** reference for a level-20 dungeon elite killed by five level-20 characters. This is a planning range, not a controlled observation. Its client build, rested state and measurement context have not been established. It belongs in `dungeonKillXpReference`, separately from `killXpEvidence` and a dungeon's population profile.

For a known elite and five known party levels, the extrapolation is:

```text
floor(soloBase(playerLevel, mobLevel)
      × anchorXpPerPlayer × 5 × playerLevel
      / (soloBase(20, 20) × sum(partyLevels)))
```

The published solo base at 20 is 145. The supplied equal-level anchor therefore returns 24–30 whole, rather than 4–6. Integer factors are multiplied before division to avoid rounding the lower anchor down to 23. Each physical kill updates all party members together; conservative enclosures retain uncertain payouts through level crossings and later hand-ins.

Exact compatible controlled observations take priority, followed by an explicitly supplied dungeon multiplier, including zero. The owner reference is used only when neither supplies the payout. It adds no second elite multiplier, dungeon quest coefficient, party bonus or approximately 20% patch bonus. Explicit XP multipliers apply once to estimates and never alter observed final payouts. Outdoor XP is unaffected. Unknown levels/ranks, normal creatures, unknown party levels, other party sizes and possible boosting suppression remain unresolved; the reference does not silently manufacture those contexts.

Tests cover the anchor, level extrapolation, unequal levels, exclusions/cap, observation and profile priority, invalid reference contexts, and independently enumerated synchronized outcomes inside a party enclosure. The synchronized check does not claim to enumerate every unequal-party outcome.
